# Devbox native resilience 0.8.0

This release repairs native research evidence quality and consolidates local X11
capture in C++23. Full and core-only archives remain available for qualified targets.

- Main article/post isolation excludes related recommendations and hidden
  advertising. Ambiguous collections do not supply misattributed prose.
- Index/teaser pages and near copies no longer inflate substantive source targets.
  Excluded documents remain inspectable; clustering does not claim factual agreement.
- Explicit-URL-only research no longer expands links or structured product URLs.
  Expansion-enabled research prioritizes in-content citations and records provenance.
- Unicode-aware excerpts use code-point units and original-text offsets. Ledger and
  detail modes honour query/excerpt controls; exclusions and candidates are pageable.
- Old extraction caches are invalidated, and historical ledgers are marked explicitly.
- Local X11 display/program/process-tree capture uses native XCB without screenshot
  utilities, XTEST or focus changes. Existing computer-use input safeguards remain.
- Narrower Boost includes reduce measured preprocessing work. Paired compression
  measurements retain libdeflate's fast path alongside streaming zlib.

[dependency tradeoffs](../DEPENDENCY_RESILIENCE.md) and
[core installation](../CORE_SERVER.md). Production deployment is a separate step.
